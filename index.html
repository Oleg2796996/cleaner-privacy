Privacy Policy — Cleaner (ru.sensify.iphonecleaner)

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Cleaner (the "app", "we") respects your privacy. This policy describes what data is processed when you use the app.

In short

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All processing happens on your device.

Photo library access

The app requests access to your photo library solely to: - find duplicate, similar, blurry photos and old screenshots; - compress large videos; - delete the items you select.

The choice is always yours: the app never deletes or modifies anything without your explicit confirmation. Photos and videos deleted through the app go to the system "Recently Deleted" album and can be restored within 30 days using iOS.

Data never leaves your device

The app has no network access: it never connects to the internet and contains no analytics, trackers, ads, or third-party SDKs. No data — photos, metadata, usage statistics — is ever sent to any server or made available to the developer.

Children

The app does not collect data from any users, including children, and contains no age-restricted content.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address. Material privacy-related changes are not expected: data collection is absent by the app's architecture.
